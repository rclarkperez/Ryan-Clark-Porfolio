01 / A little about me
Ryan Clark

I’m curious by nature
and like to build things.

I found programming after being furloughed from my hospital financial assistance job during the COVID-19 pandemic. I was looking for a new direction, went back to school, and discovered that I really enjoyed building software. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Oregon State University and now work as a full-stack engineer at Tandem Diabetes Care.

These days I work mostly with C#/.NET, React, TypeScript, SQL Server, Azure, and automated tests. Away from the computer, I’m usually making coffee, going for a run, or spending time outside with my daughter.
